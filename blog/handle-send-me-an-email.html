How to Handle "Send Me an Email" on a Cold Call

September 7, 2026 · By Flynn James · 5 min read

"Send me an email" is perhaps the most common objection in cold calling. It's often a polite way to end the conversation, but it can also be a genuine request for more information. Knowing how to handle it effectively can make the difference between losing a prospect and creating an opportunity.

Why Prospects Say "Send Me an Email"

Understanding the underlying reasons can help you respond more effectively:

  • Genuine Interest: They want more information
  • Hedge: They're not ready to commit but don't want to say no
  • Time Constraint: They don't have time to talk right now
  • Default Response: It's an automatic response to sales calls

Effective Response Strategies

Strategy 1: The Confirmation Approach

Response: "Absolutely, I'll send that over. But before I do, can I ask a quick question to make sure I send the right information?"

This approach validates their request while creating an opportunity to continue the conversation.

Strategy 2: The Value-First Approach

Response: "I'd love to send that over. To give you the most relevant info, what's your biggest challenge right now with [specific area]?"

This shows you're focused on their needs, not just selling.

Strategy 3: The Time-Frame Approach

Response: "Sure thing. Would it be helpful to have a quick 10-minute call this week to walk through it rather than a long email? I can share more context in the call."

This offers an alternative that could lead to a meeting.

Strategy 4: The Specific Question Approach

Response: "I'll email you right after this. Just so I include the right case studies, what's your current process for [topic]?"

This keeps the conversation going and gathers valuable qualification information.

What NOT to Say

  • "Let me send you a link." (Too vague)
  • "Sure, I'll email you." (Ends conversation)
  • "Can I call you back?" (May not work)

Following Up After Sending the Email

If the prospect asked for an email, follow up strategically:

  • Send a personalized email within 1 hour
  • Reference the phone conversation
  • Include a clear call-to-action
  • Follow up via phone if no response in 3-4 days

Conclusion

Handling "send me an email" effectively is a skill that can significantly improve your cold calling results. By implementing these strategies, you can turn a potential brush-off into an opportunity for deeper conversation.
