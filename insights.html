02 / The Outbound Playbook

How we build
predictable pipeline.

Tested outbound principles distilled from 11+ years on the sales floor, delivering 30+ qualified meetings every single month across global markets.

01 / COLD OPENING

Earn the first 20 seconds.

Lead with an acute business trigger, not an unprompted company bio. Immediate context generates permission to qualify.

02 / DISCOVERY & BANT

Qualify before pitching.

Uncover decision-making authority, real operational pain, and commercial timing before ever pitching product capabilities.

03 / SHOW-UP RATES

Lock in firm meeting ownership.

A calendar invite is not the conclusion. Confirm value expectation, send calendar context, and establish mutual accountability.

04 / DIALER VELOCITY

Combine volume with precision.

Power dialers (500+ dials/day) only create revenue when paired with crisp conversational agility and instant objection pivots.

05 / SDR COACHING

Coach the micro-behavior.

Focus 1-on-1 feedback on one repeatable call inflection point—such as the transition from opener to discovery question.

06 / CRM DISCIPLINE

Data accuracy equals revenue forecast.

Flawless CRM notes and lifecycle stage tracking eliminate lost leads and increase AE closing velocity.
